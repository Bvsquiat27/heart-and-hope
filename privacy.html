Privacy policy

Heart and Hope · Last updated October 4, 2026

Heart and Hope is a companion for moms and moms-to-be. You can look up life-affirming pregnancy help, light a soft ember on a night map, read and post notes on a Hope Board, and use tools such as a contraction timer, a baby tracker, and a payday budget.

This app is not emergency care. It is not medical, legal, or financial advice. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number. In the United States, you can call or text 988 for emotional crisis support.

Some features stay on your phone. An account, the Ember map, and the Hope Board send information to a server. This page says which is which.

Email angel.feliciano2k16@gmail.com to ask Heart and Hope to delete your account and the data saved with it.

In the message, use the email address on the account. Ask Heart and Hope to delete that account, including the email, baby tracking, contraction log, reminder settings, and ember record saved with it.

This email is a request. Heart and Hope does not delete the account from this page, and the app does not delete it on its own. This page does not say how long a request takes.

What stays on your phone

Unless you create an account and sign in, these stay on your phone:

  • Contraction timing
  • Baby tracking, such as feeds, diapers, and sleep
  • Reminder settings
  • The payday budget
  • An after-loss journal and check-ins
  • Notes on the “I’m scared” path and the first-90-days checklist
  • A resume and a job-email draft
  • The last city or ZIP you typed while looking for help
  • A Hope Board display name, if you type one

The app can save pages you have already opened so they open again without a connection. That copy stays on your phone.

If you allow notifications, the phone can show a reminder, or a short notice that someone left a note on your ember. That notice does not include the words of the note.

Heart and Hope keeps these on-phone items in the app’s local storage. There is no cookie banner.

Help search

Help search uses a city or ZIP you type. The directory is stored in the app. It is life-affirming pregnancy help, from public Option Line (Heartbeat International) and Birthright listings. It does not list abortion clinics. The search runs on your phone.

You can also allow the phone’s location so the app can sort nearby centers. For help search, that location stays on your phone. It is not uploaded as part of the search.

Messages you draft

The app can draft a message from what you type, such as your first name, a phone number or email, a city or ZIP, what you need, and a note. That draft is not sent to the Heart and Hope server. A drafted message is not sent until you send it yourself from your email or text app, or you copy it and send it.

Accounts

You can create an account with an email and a password. The app sends that email and password to the Heart and Hope server so it can create the account or sign you in. The server stores your email. It stores a coded form of the password, not the password itself.

A sign-in token is saved on your phone. In this version, that token lasts up to 90 days unless you sign out.

When you are signed in, the app also sends a private copy of your baby tracking, contraction log, reminder settings, and a small ember record to the server, saved with your account. The ember record is an id, a state, and how long the light stays on. That private copy is not placed on the public Ember map or the Hope Board.

You can use the app without an account. In that guest mode, contraction timing, baby tracking, and reminders stay on the phone only.

You can sign out. Signing out removes the sign-in token from the phone.

If you ask to reset your password, the server emails a link and a code to that address when a mail service is set up. The link and code expire in 30 minutes. The pages in the app do not name which mail service is used.

The app has no button that deletes an account on the server. To ask Heart and Hope to delete your account, use Delete your Heart and Hope account above.

Ember

Ember can show a light on a night map. You choose 8 hours or 24 hours. You can turn the light off sooner. The server removes the light when that time ends, or when you turn it off. It will not keep a light longer than 48 hours.

The map does not show your home address. You type a city or ZIP, or you allow the phone’s location. The app uses that to place a rough dot, and it shifts the point before sending it. Other people see a glowing dot and a state. The public map does not show your name or a ZIP.

The server stores the rough coordinates, the state, when the light expires, and a ZIP used to place the dot. That ZIP can be one you typed, or a nearby ZIP chosen from the phone’s location. Notes people send to a light are stored with that light. They are not shown on the public map. The person who lit the ember can read those notes. The secret used to open your own notes stays on your phone. Signing out removes that secret from the phone.

Hope Board

The Hope Board lets people post notes that others can read. When you post, the app sends the message and a display name to the server. If you leave the name blank, the post says “A mom.” Anyone who opens the Hope Board can read the posts. The app blocks contact details, links, and some harmful words before a post goes out.

The board keeps up to 200 posts. When it is full, older posts can be removed to make room. The app does not state a retention period, in days or months, for a Hope Board post.

The display name you type is also saved on your phone so the box can be filled in next time.

Listing flags

If you flag a directory listing because you believe abortions are provided there, the app sends the listing’s name, city, state, and id to the server for review. It also sends a short code made from the network address, not the address itself. A flag does not remove the listing by itself. The app does not state a retention period for a flag.

Ads

There are no ads in this app. Heart and Hope does not include an ad network, and it does not send your information to an advertiser.

The app loads two Firebase script files from Google. Firebase storage is turned off in the app’s settings, so those scripts are not used to save your account, Ember, or Hope Board information. That information goes to the Heart and Hope server at hearth-ember-api.hearthandhope.workers.dev, a Cloudflare Worker.

This copy of the app does not include an analytics product.

How long information is kept

  • An Ember light, and the notes on it, are removed when you turn the light off or when the time you chose ends. That time is never longer than 48 hours.
  • A password-reset link and code expire after 30 minutes.
  • A sign-in token expires after 90 days, or when you sign out.
  • The Hope Board keeps at most 200 posts. Older posts can drop off when new ones are added. The app does not state a retention period for those posts.
  • The app does not state a retention period for accounts, the private copy of baby tracking, contraction logs, reminder settings, or listing flags.
