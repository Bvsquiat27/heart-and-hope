Our promise

We honor moms and protect life. You are never a problem to fix.

Dignity for women

We never shame past choices. No “you should have…” — only next steps that respect you.

Support for life

We support life as Christ intended: practical help, community, and hope.

“For you formed my inward parts; you knitted me together in my mother’s womb. I praise you, for I am fearfully and wonderfully made.”

— Psalm 139:13–14

No abortion referrals

We do not list, link to, or refer to Planned Parenthood or any abortion clinic. Period.

Your privacy

This prototype keeps everything on your device. We draft messages you approve; your email or SMS app does the sending. We don’t upload your form to a server.

Important disclaimer

Hearth & Hope provides general information and connection tools only. It is not a substitute for medical care, legal advice, or emergency services. Always talk with a qualified clinician about your health. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 (or your local emergency number).

Local centers come from public Option Line (Heartbeat International) and Birthright directories (retrieved September 2026). Confirm hours by phone. More verified listings may be added over time. Enter any town or ZIP to find the nearest centers.

Install this app

Add Hearth & Hope to your phone’s home screen for a calm, full-screen experience and offline access to pages you’ve opened.

  • iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen → Add
  • Android (Chrome): Menu ⋮ → Install app / Add to Home screen — or use the Install banner when it appears
